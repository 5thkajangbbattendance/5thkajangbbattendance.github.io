# Page override: Parade HQ (staff app)

Applies to `app.html` (built to `attendance/`) and the Inventory app (`inventory-app/`, built to `inventory/`). Everything not listed here follows `../MASTER.md`.

- **Style:** Minimalism & Swiss. Density is a step tighter than the website:
  - card padding 20px (16px on phones)
  - grid gaps 16px
  - page title 28px Lexend
- **Menu:** Night Blue side menu on desktop. The current page is marked by a 4px Badge Gold bar and a lighter background. Phones keep the 5-tab bottom bar, with the current tab's icon and label in Brigade Blue (Dark Gold in the menu sheet).
- **Roll marking:**
  - Status buttons are at least 44px tall with 8px gaps.
  - The chosen status fills with its status colour and keeps its word.
  - No confirmation step.
- **Stat cards:**
  - Label, then number, then note. The number is Atkinson 700 at 36px (30px on phones), tabular.
  - The key stat (Attendance rate) sits on Night Blue with the number in Dark Gold.
- **Charts:** Brigade Blue bars, with the latest parade in Badge Gold. Dashed gridlines, and Atkinson 13px labels.
- **Calendar and date strip:**
  - Date numbers are Atkinson 700.
  - Parade days have a 2px Brigade Blue outline.
  - The chosen day is filled Brigade Blue.
  - Today gets a Badge Gold underline.
- **Tables:** Mist header row with 14px/700 Slate labels. Numbers are right-aligned and tabular. Rows are 48px or taller.
