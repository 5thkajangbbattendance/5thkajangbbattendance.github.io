# Page override: public website

Applies to `site.html` (built to `index.html`), `notices.html`, `form.html`, `uniform.html` and `uniform/receipt.html`. Everything not listed here follows `../MASTER.md`.

- **Style:** Accessible & Ethical, with soft corners. Sections have 64px vertical padding (48px on phones) and alternate between white and Hall Grey.
- **Hero:**
  - A real company photo under a Night Blue overlay at 72%.
  - The heading in white Lexend 700.
  - One plain sentence about when and where we meet.
  - Two buttons: "Join us" (Accent, Badge Gold) and a white-outline secondary button.
  - No slogans and no stat strip.
- **Headings:** website section titles are Lexend 700 at 36px, left-aligned, with no label above them.
- **Numbers on the website:** age ranges, event date tiles, prices and order totals use Atkinson 700 with tabular figures.
- **Forms:**
  - Visible labels above every field.
  - 48px fields on phones.
  - Errors next to the field.
  - A line saying what the details are used for.
- **Receipt:** print-first. Brigade Blue header band with a gold rule, an Atkinson order number and total, and status badges with words.
